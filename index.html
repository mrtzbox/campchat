Tent Chat Support

Chat with your whole camp. No signal needed.

Getting started

  1. Install Tent Chat on every phone in your group.
  2. Open it and choose offline only or online features on. Set your name in Profile → Edit.
  3. Stay within Bluetooth range (about 30–300 ft). Phones relay for each other, so spread out. Nearby → How it works explains every way Tent Chat connects.

Tips

  • Keep Tent Chat open, or just lock your phone. Don't swipe it away in the app switcher, or it stops relaying.
  • Phones in pockets, backpacks or tents have shorter range. Hold them out or set them up high.
  • Use Base Camp Relay (Settings) on a spare phone in the middle of camp for more range.
  • Hold the orange mic for walkie-talkie. Live audio plays when Tent Chat is open on screen.
  • Turn on Use Wi-Fi (Settings) so nearby iPhones also link over phone-to-phone Wi-Fi for more range and faster photos. No internet is used.

Long range (optional)

With a Heltec V3 LoRa radio board, Tent Chat can carry messages miles instead of feet. Tap the antenna button on the home screen to connect a board. The board can also talk to Meshtastic radios, or run as a standalone relay (hold its PRG button for 5 seconds to switch modes). Range depends on terrain; put the board up high.

Not getting notifications?

Settings → Notifications → Tent Chat: allow notifications and Time Sensitive alerts. If you wear an Apple Watch, alerts may go to the watch while your phone is locked.

Emergencies

Tent Chat only reaches phones and radios nearby. It is not an emergency service. In an emergency, use your iPhone's Emergency SOS.
