Tent Chat Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Tent Chat is an offline messenger. It is designed to collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Tent Chat has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We (the developer) never receive your messages, contacts, name, location or any other data.

How messages travel

Messages go directly between nearby phones over Bluetooth and phone-to-phone Wi-Fi and may be relayed by other nearby phones in the mesh. Wi-Fi links between iPhones are encrypted. Private chats and Chat Rooms are end-to-end encrypted, so relaying phones cannot read them. Campfire and public channel messages are public to anyone nearby.

If you use a LoRa radio board, messages also travel between your phone and the board over Bluetooth and between boards over radio. Private chats stay end-to-end encrypted. Messages on public Meshtastic channels can be read by anyone nearby with a compatible radio.

What stays on your phone

Your screen name, encryption keys, messages and settings are stored only on your device. Keys are kept in the iOS Keychain and never leave the device. App data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. "Wipe Everything" (or triple-tapping the main title) erases it all.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to send and receive messages with nearby phones.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice message or use walkie-talkie.
  • Camera: only to scan a buddy's verification QR code.
  • Photos: only when you choose a photo to share.
  • Notifications: to alert you to new messages.
  • Location: never used. Tent Chat does not request location access.

Internet

Tent Chat never connects to the internet.

Children

Tent Chat is not directed at children under 13.
